About Jacob Jeans

Jacob Jeans is a denim brand created by N. Benjamin in London, England, in 2024. Jeans were originally made in France and Italy, and denim takes its name from de Nîmes — "from Nîmes", a city in France.

Jacob Davis, a Jewish tailor, was the first to create modern jeans, and collaborated with the famous Levi Strauss.

Today Jacob Jeans aims to create the next evolution of jeans, so that the whole world may experience a fresh touch.

Nathan Benjamin — CEO & Founder
